Live VLM

A camera frame and a prompt go to Qwen3-VL-4B on the board's Ampere GPU; the answer streams back word by word. Nothing leaves the device.

In plain words

  1. Camera: the video comes from a camera feed (USB webcam, GMSL camera or IP camera) or a video file.
  2. Pick a frame: about once a second (every 30th frame) the app takes one picture and compresses it.
  3. Ask a question: the picture goes to the AI together with the prompt, e.g. “Describe what you see” or a monitoring task like Retail or Safety.
  4. The AI answers: Qwen3-VL-4B, a vision-language model, runs on the board's Ampere GPU. It looks at the picture and writes the answer.
  5. Live on screen: the answer appears word by word, with how fast it was (time to first word, words per second) and how busy the chips are.

Nothing goes to the cloud: the camera, the model and the answers all stay on this board.

Camera feedUSB webcam (default) or GMSL camera, read on the server and sent to the browser over WebRTC (aiortc). Same-machine connection, no STUN/TURN.
IP cameraRTSP stream (H.264), read on the server

2 · Frame sampling

RateEvery 30th frame by default (adjustable)
One at a timeA new frame is only sent once the previous answer is done; frames in between are skipped, so answers never queue up
EncodingShrunk to at most 1024 px, JPEG (quality 90) → base64, in an OpenAI-compatible chat request

3 · Prompt

PresetsScene understanding, monitoring (safety, retail, security…) and robot navigation, or your own text
Length“Answer in 1-2 sentences.” is added by default; max 512 tokens; temperature 0.2

4 · Model GPU

ModelQwen3-VL-4B-Instruct, GGUF Q4_K_M (4-bit weights), the same model as the IQ9 demo
RuntimeOllama with llama.cpp and CUDA; vision encoder and language model both on the Ampere GPU (~5.7 GB of the shared memory)
APIOpenAI-compatible /v1/chat/completions, streamed
LoadingThe model stays loaded for 30 minutes after the last answer; the first answer after that takes a few seconds longer

Traffic cascade

A small, fast detector looks at every frame; the large vision-language model only looks at the vehicles you pick. The detector runs on the DLA, the vision-language model on the GPU.

In plain words

  1. Video: a traffic video (or camera) plays on the board.
  2. Find vehicles: a small, fast AI model (YOLO) checks every frame for cars, trucks, buses and motorcycles, in about 25 ms per frame on the DLA, the Orin's deep-learning accelerator.
  3. Follow each vehicle: a tracker follows each one across frames and keeps its best, largest picture.
  4. Describe it: click a vehicle and the big vision-language model (Qwen3-VL-4B, on the GPU) reads its color, type, brand and, if readable, its number plate.
  5. Why two models: the small model is fast enough for every frame; the big one is slower but understands much more, so it only looks at the vehicles you ask about. Each runs on the engine that suits it, so the GPU stays free for the vision-language model.

Technical details

1 · Video

SourceDemo MP4 (highway) or an uploaded video, decoded on the server and looped
DeliveryAnnotated frames go to the browser over WebRTC

2 · Detection DLA

ModelsYOLOv8n (default) or YOLO26n, both Ultralytics exports, switchable live in Settings
RuntimeTensorRT FP16 on DLA 0 (default) or on the GPU; ONNX Runtime on the CPU
On the DLABackbone and neck run on the DLA; the detection head (8,400 candidate boxes, over the DLA's 8,192 limit) falls back to the GPU
Input640 × 640, letterboxed (aspect ratio kept)
FilterScore ≥ 0.45, NMS at IoU 0.7, then one box per object across classes
Classescar, motorcycle, bus, truck (of the 80 COCO classes)
SpeedYOLOv8n ~25 ms per frame on the DLA, ~9 ms on the GPU, ~82 ms on the CPU (incl. image preparation)

3 · Tracking & best crop

TrackerIoU matching between frames; a track is confirmed after 3 matched frames and dropped after 15 missed ones
Best cropThe largest, fully visible view of each vehicle, kept at full resolution
When shownOnce the vehicle has passed its largest size for 5 frames, so the picture is the best one

4 · Describe GPU

TriggerClick a vehicle: its full-resolution crop goes to Qwen3-VL-4B on the GPU
OutputColor, type, brand and, if readable, the number plate; max 120 tokens
SafeguardsOne description at a time; 40 s pause after a failure

Describe prompt

Highway video

Describe this vehicle in 1-2 sentences: its color, type (car/truck/bus/etc.), and brand if visible. Only mention the license plate if it is clearly legible - don't guess at characters you can't read clearly.

Editable live with the Prompt button on the traffic page.
